Download once. Read later.

Aftercast is a podcast player. Subscribe, download, and play as you would expect. When you want an episode as text, choose Transcribe on a download. The words appear while you listen, and you can read what is already finished.

Transcription runs on your phone. The audio is not uploaded.

The speech model is not part of the install. The first time you transcribe, Aftercast downloads the Whisper English model chosen in Playback settings and keeps it for later episodes. The standard choice is small English, about 375 MB. A faster model is about 160 MB, and a larger one is about 900 MB. Plan on that extra space, and on a long episode taking a while. Phones from about the last five years are the intended fit. An older phone can still run the faster or standard model. It will just take longer and use more of the phone's memory while it works. The larger model can be too heavy on an older phone while audio is playing.
